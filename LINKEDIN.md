## If you are editing a position

**Turn off "Notify network" before saving.** These are existing jobs, not new
ones, and the announcement would be misleading. When LinkedIn offers to post
about it afterwards, choose Skip.

**Saving a position resets the headline** to the short automatic version. Put
the full one back afterwards and check it.
